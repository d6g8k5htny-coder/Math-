**What it is.** The four junction sliver cells of a narrow-regime node are formed by `ddvq._vcell` from decimal endpoints `[a, b]` with the centre rounded down and the half-width rounded up, so a cell always covers `a` but can stop short of `b` by up to `10⁻²⁸` (a few units in the 30th digit).
