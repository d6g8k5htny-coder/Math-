## What the chain states (paraphrase; the notes govern)

The setting is [P]'s: the centered variance-one Gaussian field on the torus `ℝ³/(Lℤ³)`, two pins `M_r = −(r/2)u` and `S_r = (r/2)u` at heights `b` and `b − kr³` with zero gradients, the regression law `Q_r`, the weight `W_r = |det H_M det H_S|1{H_M < 0, ind H_S = 2}`, the tilted law `Q_r^W`, and C96's elder event `H_r`. The soft layer is `D_Λ = {|λ̃| ≤ Λ}`, with `λ̃ = kλ₁/r` the rescaled soft transverse eigenvalue and `λ₂` the hard one.

- **A3 (every `d ≥ 3`, deterministic).** Under explicit hard-barrel conditions (B1)–(B3) on a product barrel `Ω × B̄_ε`, the reduced height `G(x) = max_{|z|≤ε} g(x, z)` is `C²`, `O(q₀²/Λ)`-close to the planar section, with an explicit Hessian bound (Lemma SR). Composed with the corrected planar trap (A2, C95) and C97's chord, this gives the elder certificate QS-E′_d and the rejected certificate QS-R_d at fixed planar tolerances.
- **A3.1 (every `d ≥ 3`, deterministic).** The torus lift (TL) of the chart maximin; Lemma N_d and Corollary H_d, which identify the H0 partner of `M_r` on [P] §8's Morse locus (under QS-E′_d it is `S_r`; under QS-R_d with `W_r > 0` it is not); Lemma FL.1′ and Lemma SR′, which make the reduced height `O(r)`-close to the planar cubic in `C²`; Theorem C_d, an order-`r` window certificate; the raw radii `ρ_E`, `ρ_R` of §6.
