## What the chain states (paraphrase; the notes govern)

The setting is [P]'s: the centered variance-one Gaussian field on the torus `ℝ³/(Lℤ³)`, two pins `M_r = −(r/2)u` and `S_r = (r/2)u` at heights `b` and `b − kr³` with zero gradients, the regression law `Q_r`, the weight `W_r = |det H_M det H_S|1{H_M < 0, ind H_S = 2}`, the tilted law `Q_r^W`, and C96's elder event `H_r`. The soft layer is `D_Λ = {|λ̃| ≤ Λ}`, with `λ̃ = kλ₁/r` the rescaled soft transverse eigenvalue and `λ₂` the hard one.

- **A3 (every `d ≥ 3`, deterministic).** Under explicit hard-barrel conditions (B1)–(B3) on a product barrel `Ω × B̄_ε`, the reduced height `G(x) = max_{|z|≤ε} g(x, z)` is `C²`, `O(q₀²/Λ)`-close to the planar section, with an explicit Hessian bound (Lemma SR). Composed with the corrected planar trap (A2, C95) and C97's chord, this gives the elder certificate QS-E′_d and the rejected certificate QS-R_d at fixed planar tolerances.
- **A3.1 (every `d ≥ 3`, deterministic).** The torus lift (TL) of the chart maximin; Lemma N_d and Corollary H_d, which identify the H0 partner of `M_r` on [P] §8's Morse locus (under QS-E′_d it is `S_r`; under QS-R_d with `W_r > 0` it is not); Lemma FL.1′ and Lemma SR′, which make the reduced height `O(r)`-close to the planar cubic in `C²`; Theorem C_d, an order-`r` window certificate; the raw radii `ρ_E`, `ρ_R` of §6.
- **A3.2 (every `d ≥ 3`, deterministic).** Lemma WF: the exact factorization of `W_r` in the window chart through Schur complements and Haynsworth inertia; Corollary WF: its explicit form under Theorem C_d's hypotheses; and a map of what a `d ≥ 3` rate would need, row by row against the planar chain.
- **A3.3 (`d = 3`, deterministic).** Proposition W3: for a pinned `C⁴` field with `0 < r ≤ 1` and `rN ≤ 1`, `|W_r/r⁴ − (λ₂)₊²w| ≤ C rN(|λ₂| + rN)Π⁴(1 + |λ₂|)²` pathwise, uniform as the hard eigenvalue `λ₂ → 0` and across its sign change. The branch `rN > 1` is outside W3's premise; A3.4's Lemma D handles it separately, with Lemma P's crude pathwise bound on the window Hessians. Corollary TE on the typed sets; the step-1 window identity. The K5 control's unordered witness is recorded as such in the successor text, with an ordered witness (Astra's) in the addendum control.
- **A3.4 (`d = 3`; conditional on A3.3).** Lemma CM₃ (the 20 contact jets are uniformly nondegenerate; conditional moments of the field norm are polynomial), Lemma S (the spectral change of variables), Lemma D (the weight given the jets), Theorem J₃ (the soft-layer jet law at rate `r`: `∫_{D_Λ}P^q|g_r − g₀| ≤ Cr`, the normalizer, `Q_r^W(|λ̃| ≤ Λ) = r³m_Λ/z₀ + O(r⁴)`), and Corollary E₃ (the typing-edge integrals `∫φ(s)(s + r)ds`). These are C92's Theorem J and C93's (E1)–(E12) in `d = 3`.
- **A3.5 (`d = 3`; §§1–3 deterministic, §4 conditional on A3.4).** Lemma FW₃ (C91's window error bound in `d = 3`, with explicit constants, on the cube and on a barrel box), Lemma SR′_b and Proposition D₃ (the reduced height on growing windows, with the hard-gap term `C_ekN²rw⁴/λ₂`), Lemma T₃ (the normalized Hessian transfer), Lemma E₃⁺ (the joint edge–gap majorant), Corollary FW₃′ and Theorem G₃ (the analytic good event at the `r³` scale, with `Q_r^W(D_Λ ∖ G₃) ≤ Cr⁴` for `w = r^{−β}`, `0 ≤ β ≤ 3/16`, and a sufficiently large fixed `p`).
- **A3.6 (`d = 3`; M₃, SC, E₃(a), R₃(a) deterministic, the rest conditional on A3.4).** Theorem E₃: on the model elder sector the good event fails with weighted mass `O(r^{7/2})`, and on it A3's QS-E′_d decides elder pairing. Lemma SC and Theorem R₃: C97's two-margin chord, drawn in the plane of the pin axis and the soft eigenvector, certifies rejection with no barrel, no reduced height, no gradient pin and no bound on `λ₂`; the bad mass is again `O(r^{7/2})`. Theorem BL₃: `Q_r^W(D_Λ ∩ (H_r Δ E)) ≤ Cr^{7/2}` and `Q_r^W(H_r | D_Λ) = m_E/m_Λ + O(√r)`, uniformly in `b ∈ B₀` (the fixed compact height set), `k ∈ [k₋, k₊]` and the frame. ## Replay

    python3 -B -S frontiers/qs_d3_soft_layer_chain_20261005/replay.py
    python3 -B -O -S frontiers/qs_d3_soft_layer_chain_20261005/replay.py

`replay.py` uses the standard library only and takes about ten minutes per mode (the A3.3 and A3.5 scripts and the A3.3 addendum control each take about 30 s per run, and they have 17 mutant runs between them). It runs four checks:
1. **Identities.** Every stored file has the identity pinned in `SOURCES.json`, and the tree holds nothing else.
2. **Extraction.** Each control script and its stdout equal the fenced payloads in the stored controls comment, under the extraction rule those comments state (the rule of [5971055189](https://github.com/d6g8k5htny-coder/main/issues/229#issuecomment-5971055189)).
3. **The checkers.** All eight scripts reproduce their published stdout byte for byte under the current interpreter's `-O`/`-S` flags: `a3d_exact.py` (A3), `a31_exact.py` (A3.1), `a32_exact.py` (A3.2), `a33_exact.py` and the addendum control `a33_k5o.py` (A3.3), `a34_exact.py` (A3.4), `a35_exact.py` (A3.5) and `a36_exact.py` (A3.6). Their 48 mutant invocations exit 1 and their 14 invalid invocations exit 2, as the controls comments state.
4. **Negative controls.** A one-byte change to a stored note is detected, and a checker with one changed constant (A3.6's hard-gap integral coefficient `5/4` replaced by `1/4`) is rejected.

The workflow `.github/workflows/qs-d3-soft-layer-chain.yml` runs both modes, as two jobs, on Python 3.11.16. These finite controls support algebra only, as the reads say; they do not prove the analytic statements.
