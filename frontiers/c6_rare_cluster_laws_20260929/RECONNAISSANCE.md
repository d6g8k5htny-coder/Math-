# External reconnaissance — 29 September 2026

Scope: locate relevant existing probability frameworks and prevent claiming elementary Poissonization as novel. This is a narrow reconnaissance memo, not an exhaustive novelty audit or an external acceptance of the project's Gaussian estimates.

An actual Consensus search for "Gaussian random fields critical points factorial moments rare clusters Palm conditional distribution compound Poisson approximation domain:math" returned: all 30 monthly searches used, resetting October 1. No result was returned or attributed to Consensus.

Primary arXiv abstract/metadata pages were opened directly:

1. Louis Gass and Michele Stecconi, *The number of critical points of a Gaussian field: finiteness of moments*, arXiv:2305.17586v2, 19 July 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17586 . Proves finite critical-count moments under finite-jet nondegeneracy. Finiteness by itself is not the uniform rare-pinned O(r^3) input used here.
2. Louis H. Y. Chen and Aihua Xia, *Stein's method, Palm theory and Poisson process approximation*, Annals of Probability 32 (2004), 2545–2569, DOI 10.1214/009117904000000027; arXiv:math/0410169. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410169 . Existing Palm/Stein/local-dependence framework. This note does not derive a Stein bound or silently identify positivity conditioning with size bias.
3. Michael V. Boutsikas and Eutichia Vaggelatou, *A new method for obtaining sharp compound Poisson approximation error estimates for sums of locally dependent random variables*, Bernoulli 16 (2010), 301–330, DOI 10.3150/09-BEJ201; arXiv:1010.1625. https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.1625 . Existing Poisson and compound-Poisson TV approximation for independent or locally dependent nonnegative integer variables. No novel generic Poissonization method is claimed here.
