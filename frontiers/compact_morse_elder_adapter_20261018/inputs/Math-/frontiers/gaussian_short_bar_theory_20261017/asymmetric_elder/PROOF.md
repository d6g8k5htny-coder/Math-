## 3. Strong convexity supplies the asymmetric boundary

Put `g=f'`. Then `g(0)=g(s)=0`, `g'(0)=F_xx(0)<0`, and `g''>=tau/2`. Strong convexity gives `g<0` on (0,s), and positive g on [-R,0) and (s,R]. For example,

```text

0=g(0)>=-s g'(s)+tau s^2/4,      g'(s)>= tau s/4.
```

For x<=0 and x>=s respectively, Taylor's integral inequality gives

```text
g(x)>=g'(0)x+tau x^2/4>=tau x^2/4,
g(x)>=g'(s)(x-s)+tau(x-s)^2/4>=tau(x-s)^2/4.
```

