## The finding

`NOTE.md` §0 ("Consequences", second bullet) and the PR body call the manuscript's correction ratio "certified". What this
packet certifies is an enclosure of the ratio of the integral (CU.2) to `c_(3,24)`. Reading that ratio as the relative
correction `(c1/c) ell^(7/12)` of [CU] (CU.1) needs two premises that the packet consumes and does not review: Theorem CU,
and side24_v1's enclosure of `c_(3,24)`. `NOTE.md` §8 already states both limits; the §0 bullet is wider than they are.

The PR body's `I^cand` bullet has the same shape. The identification of the certified intervals with the coefficient that
Math-#218 carries comes from #218 and [CU], not from this packet.
