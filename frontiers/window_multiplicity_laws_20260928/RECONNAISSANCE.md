## Primary source actually read at theorem level

Diego Armentano, Jean-Marc Azais, Jose Rafael Leon,
*On a general Kac-Rice formula for the measure of a level set*,
https://arxiv.org/html/2304.07424v3 (5 December 2023 version).

Theorem 7.1 is the weighted expected-integral formula under Theorem 2.1 and its
stated lower-semicontinuity/conditional-law hypotheses. Theorem 2.2 and Remark 7
supply the Gaussian regression bridge; Remark 8 treats a jointly Gaussian mark
process. Section 8.1 applies the framework to critical points. Our application
uses the already source-bound pair formula in [RC], with the original endpoint
weight once and each witness determinant once. Borel height-mark extensions are
handled as finite-measure identities, not by pretending every Borel indicator is
lower semicontinuous. No theorem-number substitution is made.

## Adjacent literature — scope checked at record/abstract level

Safa Ladgham and Raphael Lachieze-Rey,
*Local repulsion of planar Gaussian critical points*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.04150 (2022 preprint; SPA publication 2023).
Its abstract concerns small-ball factorial moments and type-resolved repulsion
for stationary isotropic planar fields. It motivates near-collision analysis but

*A second moment bound for critical points of planar Gaussian fields in shrinking
height windows*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.11336 (2019 preprint; Statistics &
Probability Letters 160, 108698, 2020; DOI 10.1016/j.spl.2020.108698).
Its abstract concerns unconditioned stationary planar fields and large domains
with shrinking height windows. Its second-moment problem is relevant background;
its result is not substituted for uniform estimates under this packet's pin law.
