# Exact object mapping

Jet indices 0..8: q,a,w,z,c40,c31,c22,c13,c04. R=1+sum of all nine absolute values.
HM and HS: source section 2 endpoint Hessians. Negative: strictly negative quadratic
form on every nonzero two-vector. W: absolute determinant product restricted to
HM negative definite and det HS<0. M8: supplied integral upper bound for R^8.
Base measure in dm2_moment is arbitrary and common to every premise/conclusion.
