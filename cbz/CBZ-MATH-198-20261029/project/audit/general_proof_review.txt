Other mathematical source observations

1. Safety and palette iff
The erase-based minimalBad definition is equivalent to inclusion minimality
under decreasing. The strict-subset lemma supplies exactly the necessity proof:
a crossing minimal bad e has each block fragment proper and good; its common
palette color gives the bad constant-colored class. Sufficiency extracts a
minimal bad subset of a bad color class and handles the single-block and
crossing cases separately. paletteSeparated quantifies every minimal witness,
not a sample. Total f:X->Fin K causes no loss versus source partial colorings:
for positive K they extend, and K=0 makes both separation and color conclusions
vacuous in exactly the relevant way. Empty ground, block index or color index
cases introduce no mathematical exception. No separate error was found.

2. Amalgamation apart from finding1
The proof chooses actual decompositions separately for U intersect block i,
injects positive local labels into actual integer palettes, and constructs
allowed local color classes as subsets of chosen good parts. The global
color-class family is disjoint and has union U, so the safety iff applies.
The proof only needs the local-cover hypothesis on U intersect block i; the
proposed restricted-domain correction fits the proof and original source.
Support remains explicit in the source wrapper; it is redundant for the
stronger safety helper because a used generator already lies in that block.

3. Actual probability and capped budgets
localMass sums blockWeight over subsets of the actual block; it does not
mistake ambient muOf(localFamily) for the restricted-coordinate law. The dummy
RealizedData carries only partition coordinates and invokes algebraic finite
identities that do not assume capacity validity. Downward closure gives global
goodness => all block restrictions good. The product identity uses disjoint
coordinate blocks, with no independence assertion about crossing witnesses.
Positive global mass implies positive local masses before ordinary logarithms
are taken. Local masses are in[0,1]; zero local mass forces zero global mass;
empty blocks have local mass1. Probability endpoints p_v=0 or1 are included.
The family-price union inequality correctly deduplicates generators and keeps

The weighted Holder proof assigns residual weight to the constant1. Its
