# L-SHRINKING-EXCLUSION — Spatial exclusion shrinking with r

**Status in this map:** open lemma.

## Target

Replace the fixed remote exclusion `dist(x,0) ≥ ρ > 0` by an exclusion radius that may tend to zero with `r` (mesoscopic / near-pin remote region).

## Tasks to close

1. Identify the scaling regime(s) between fixed annulus and fixed remote (`r ≪ distance ≪ ρ`).
2. Prove covariance nondegeneracy and regression bounds uniformly down to that shrinking cutoff.
3. Bound the three-determinant numerator (or intensity) with constants allowed to depend on the cutoff law.
4. Stitch to [T-FIXED-REMOTE](../closed-theorems/T-FIXED-REMOTE.md) and annulus closed theorems.

## Notes

Theorem A explicitly forbids shrinking `ρ` with `r`.
