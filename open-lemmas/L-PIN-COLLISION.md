# L-PIN-COLLISION — Pin / witness collision control

**Status in this map:** open lemma.

## Target

Expected-count or probability bounds when a critical-point witness approaches a pin, or when two witnesses approach each other, inside the coalescence scale.

## Tasks to close

1. Define the collision neighborhoods excluded from remote-window and annulus statements.
2. Prove either a separate bound on those neighborhoods or a packing/collision lemma reducing multiple witnesses to separated ones.
3. Show compatibility with endpoint weights and the full normalizer `Z_r`.

## Notes

Remote-window §6 treats mutually separated witnesses; coincident witnesses remain outside scope.
