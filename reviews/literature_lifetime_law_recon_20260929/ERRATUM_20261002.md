## What the source says

D. Perez, *On the persistent homology of almost surely `C⁰` stochastic processes*, arXiv:2012.09459v3, §1.1:

> In this paper, we will be concerned with almost surely `C⁰` processes, but it is noteworthy that the study of smooth
> random fields (and their topology) is currently an open field of research in probability theory.

This is a general remark about the topology of smooth random fields. It does not single out the short-bar
(small-lifetime) density of smooth fields, or any particular asymptotic, as an open problem. Nor does it bear on whether
such a law is new.

## Corrected reading

> Pérez (arXiv:2012.09459v3, §1.1) describes smooth random-field topology as an open research area; that general
> observation does not identify this particular short-bar asymptotic. The searches recorded in this memo did not locate
> the formula and do not establish novelty.

The rest of the memo, its neighbour table otherwise, and `PROTOCOL.json` are unchanged. Pérez's results remain the
closest one-dimensional neighbours for rough paths. They concern a different regularity class and a different counting
convention, and they do not conflict with the smooth-field law.
