# L-HAZARD-INTERP — Hazard interpolation lemma

**Status in this map:** closed lemma (completed proof in Theorem F package §2).

## Statement

For a proper decreasing family containing the empty set, separate coordinatewise concavity of the hazard under exponential tilting yields

`-log μ_p(A) ≥ H_A ∏ φ(p_i)`,

and therefore any transformed prices `c_i ≤ φ(p_i)` satisfy the full-ground generator price bound (F8).
